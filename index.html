About Me

Hi! I am a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where I am fortunate to be advised by Manolis Vlatakis-Gkaragkounis. My current research focuses on theoretical machine learning, especially game theory and online learning. I am particularly interested in understanding how learning algorithms behave in strategic environments, and what strategic players can and cannot achieve when interacting with them.

Before my PhD, I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at Colby College. There, I worked with Evan Randles on random walks using electrical networks, and with Hannen Wolfe on interactive emotive sounds for robots. I was awarded the Marston Morse Prize in Mathematics at Colby. I am open to research collaborations and internship opportunities.
